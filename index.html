Connect this screen to ClickUp

  1. In ClickUp, click your profile picture (top right) and open Settings.
  2. Open Apps and copy your API token. It starts with pk_.
  3. Paste it below and tap Connect.

The key is saved on this device only and is sent only to ClickUp. Anyone who uses this device can see the answers. Tap Forget key in Settings when the festival is over.
